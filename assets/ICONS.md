## Why Excel and LinkedIn are placeholders

Simple Icons 16.35.0 no longer ships Power BI, Microsoft Excel or LinkedIn. The official Power BI
mark was available from Microsoft's own repository (above). The official Excel and LinkedIn marks are
only published through Microsoft's and LinkedIn's brand portals, which could not be reached when this
was built, so neutral glyphs stand in. Replace them with the official downloads from those portals
if you want the real marks.

Brand names and logos belong to their owners. They are used here only to label the tools and
platforms in use and do not imply endorsement.
